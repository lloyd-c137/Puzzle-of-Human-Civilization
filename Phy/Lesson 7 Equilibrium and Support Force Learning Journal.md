### Learning record — 2026-09-03 — Parallelogram-rule experiment data

#### question

这是我们刚刚做的一个实验的数据记录，我们把一个皮筋两头固定，将两根细线系在皮筋的一端作为点O，然后使用两个弹簧测力仪从垂直方向拉它，让皮筋的点O从原位置移动到平行四边形的一个顶点，然后我们以该点为平行四边形的一个顶点，两个力的方向为平行四边形的两个边做平行四边形，最后求出合力为2.13N，分析误差得到实际准确值为2.1N

#### respond

The experiment uses a fixed rubber band, a point O, and two spring scales pulling in perpendicular directions. The two measured forces are represented as the adjacent sides of a parallelogram, and the diagonal represents the resultant force. The measured resultant is 2.13 N; according to the learner's error analysis, the accepted value is 2.1 N. The difference is 0.03 N, corresponding to an approximate relative difference of 1.4%. The method is appropriate provided the two force directions are perpendicular and the drawing scale is consistent.

![Parallelogram-rule experiment data record]({{ '/assets/images/physics/lesson-6-parallelogram-experiment.jpg' | relative_url }})

#### reflection

The learner connected the physical setup to the geometric vector model: the spring-scale readings provide the force magnitudes, the pulling directions provide the vector directions, and the diagonal gives the resultant. The result is close to the accepted value, but the Journal should keep the measured value 2.13 N separate from the error-adjusted value 2.1 N. Possible uncertainty sources include reading the spring scales, drawing the parallelogram, and making the directions exactly perpendicular.

### Learning record — 2026-09-03 — Why support force is not always equal to weight

#### respond

The intended idea is close, but the wording is incorrect. It should be “支持力不能总写成重力，因为支持力不总是等于重力.” On a horizontal surface at rest with no other vertical forces, support force equals weight. If an additional downward hand force acts, the support force is larger; if another upward force acts, it can be smaller. Assessment: 4/10.

#### reflection

The learner is recognizing that support force depends on the complete vertical-force situation, but has confused the names of the two quantities in the sentence. The key correction is to distinguish gravity/weight from support force. This message supplements Checkout Question 2; Checkout Question 3 remains unanswered.

### Learning record — 2026-09-03 — Understanding the two-scale question

#### question

重 600N的人静止站在两台水平放置的秤上，左秤读数是220N，且没有扶墙或其他支撑。右秤读数是多少？这个人所受的合力是多少？请分别说明两种数值代表的物理量。 这是什么意思

#### respond

The question gives a person’s weight as 600 N downward and the left scale’s upward support as 220 N. The right scale’s reading is unknown. Because the person is standing still and has no other support, the upward support from both scales balances the downward weight, so the setup is (F_{\text{left}}+F_{\text{right}}-600=0). It asks for both the right scale reading and the person’s net force, then asks you to distinguish an individual support-force reading from the vector sum of all forces.

#### reflection

The learner needs the physical meaning of each number before calculating: 600 N is weight, 220 N is one scale’s support force, the right-scale value is the other support force, and net force describes the total balance. The key condition is “静止且没有其他支撑,” which makes the total net force zero.

### Learning record — 2026-09-03 — Free-body diagram for a hanging lamp

#### question

1. 一盏重 24 N 的灯由竖直细绳悬挂且保持静止。画出灯的受力图，求绳对灯的拉力和灯的合力。为什么“合力为零”不等于“每一个力都为零”？ 这个题目是不是需要画拉力 和灯的重力

#### respond

Yes. The free-body diagram should show the tension exerted by the rope on the lamp upward and the gravitational force exerted by Earth on the lamp downward. Because the lamp is stationary, the two forces are equal in magnitude: tension (24\ \mathrm N) upward and weight (24\ \mathrm N) downward, giving a net force of (0\ \mathrm N). The forces are not individually zero; they cancel in the vector sum.

#### reflection

The learner correctly identifies the two forces that act on the hanging lamp and is ready to draw the diagram. The important labeling habit is to write who exerts each force on the lamp, not only “拉力” and “重力.” The next step is to draw the arrows and explain why their sum is zero.
