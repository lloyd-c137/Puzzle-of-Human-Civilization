### Learning record — 2026-09-02 — Initial prediction: adding forces

#### question

如果两个力同向，合力的大小为两个力之和，方向为两个力的相同方向 如果方向相反，那么合力为大力减去小力 方向为大力的方向 如果互相垂直，合力为以两个力的方向为边的三角形的斜边的大小，方向为斜边的方向

#### respond

The answer is mostly correct. Same-direction forces add, and opposite-direction forces subtract with the resultant pointing toward the larger force. For perpendicular forces, the resultant is the parallelogram diagonal; because the angle is 90°, it can also be treated as the hypotenuse of a right triangle and calculated with the Pythagorean theorem. Assessment: 8.5/10.

#### reflection

The learner understands the basic vector-addition rules. The main precision point is that the triangle is formed by placing the two perpendicular vectors head-to-tail, while the general construction is the parallelogram rule. The next step is to explain the result using a numerical perpendicular-force example and state why magnitudes cannot always be added directly.

### Learning record — 2026-09-02 — Net force and balanced forces

#### question

1. 小车同时受到向右 12 N 和向左 5 N 的水平力。合力是多少、指向哪里？若向左的力变为 12 N，合力为零是否意味着小车没有受到力？合力是7N，指向右，

#### respond

The answer is correct. With 12 N to the right and 5 N to the left, the resultant is 7 N to the right. If the leftward force becomes 12 N, the two forces still act on the cart, but their vector sum is 0 N; they cancel in the net-force calculation. Assessment: 9.5/10.

#### reflection

### Learning record — 2026-09-02 — Parallelogram and triangle methods

#### question

2. 同一物体受到向东 6 N、向北 8 N 的两个力。画出平行四边形并求合力大小，标明合力方向。为什么不能将大小直接写成 14 N？为什么是平行四边形，为什么不是三角形

#### respond

The parallelogram rule is used when both force vectors start from the same point: copy each vector from the tip of the other, forming a parallelogram; the diagonal from the common starting point is the resultant. A triangle method is also valid when the vectors are placed head-to-tail: the resultant is the third side from the start of the first vector to the end of the second. For perpendicular 6 N and 8 N forces, the triangle is a right triangle and gives (\sqrt{6^2+8^2}=10\ \mathrm N). The parallelogram is made of two identical such triangles, so both methods give the same resultant. Assessment: question shows productive conceptual checking.

#### reflection

The learner is checking whether the drawing rule is a fixed geometric shape or a representation choice. The key understanding is that vector addition can be shown as a parallelogram with common tails or as a head-to-tail triangle; the resultant itself is unchanged. The next focus is to draw both constructions and compare the same diagonal/resultant.

The conclusion that the resultant is not always 20 N is correct, and the perpendicular case is a valid counterexample in principle. #### reflection

The learner understands that the resultant depends on direction and has identified perpendicular forces as a counterexample. The main errors are reversing same/opposite-direction rules and dropping a factor of 10 in the perpendicular calculation. Next focus is to state three cases clearly: same direction \(20\ \mathrm N\), opposite direction \(0\ \mathrm N\), and perpendicular direction \(14.1\ \mathrm N\).
