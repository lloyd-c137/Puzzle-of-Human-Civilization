## Key knowledge and vocabulary

| Concept or term | My current explanation | Example or connection |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Natural motion | In Aristotle’s model, motion an object performs according to its nature. | A stone falling toward its natural place. |
| Forced motion | In Aristotle’s model, motion caused by an external push or pull. | A cart moving because a person pushes it. |
| Newton’s first law | If the net force is zero, an object remains at rest or continues with constant velocity. | Without friction, a rolling ball would continue moving. |
| Friction | A force that opposes relative motion between surfaces. | Rough ground slows a rolling ball. |
| Velocity vector | A quantity with magnitude and direction. | A boat’s across-river velocity combines with the river’s downstream velocity. |
| Resultant velocity | The single velocity vector produced by adding velocity vectors. | Perpendicular (6\ \mathrm{m/s}) and (8\ \mathrm{m/s}) velocities give (10\ \mathrm{m/s}). |
