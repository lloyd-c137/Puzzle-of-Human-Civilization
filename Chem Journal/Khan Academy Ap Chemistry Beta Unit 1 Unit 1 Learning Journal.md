## Key knowledge and vocabulary

| Concept or term | My current explanation | Example or connection |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Average atomic mass | Multiply each isotope mass by its fractional abundance, then add the weighted contributions. The result describes an abundance-weighted average, not the mass of each individual atom. | The hypothetical X calculation gives 10.8 u although individual atoms are either the 10.0 u or 11.0 u isotope. |
| Mole and Avogadro's number | A mole is a counting amount; multiply moles by Avogadro's number to find the number of particles. | 0.500 mol corresponds to about 3.01 × 10²³ molecules. |
| Molar mass | The mass of one mole of a substance, calculated by adding the atomic masses according to the formula subscripts. | Al₂O₃: 2 Al atoms plus 3 O atoms gives 101.96 g/mol. |
| Mass-to-particle conversion | First divide mass by molar mass to obtain moles, then multiply by Avogadro's number to obtain atoms or molecules. | 500.0 g Fe → 8.953 mol Fe → 5.391 × 10²⁴ Fe atoms. |
| Molecular formula and subscripts | A subscript gives the number of atoms of that element in each molecule and multiplies that element's atomic mass in the molar-mass sum. | C₆H₈O₆ contains 6 C, 8 H, and 6 O atoms per molecule. |
| Significant figures in conversions | Keep guard digits during intermediate calculations and round the reported result according to the limiting measured value. | 3.60 g glucose gives 0.0200 mol and 1.20 × 10²² molecules, each to 3 significant figures. |

### Learning record — 2026-10-21 — Exercise 37: average atomic mass and isotope abundance

#### question

把四种同位素质量分别乘各自百分比再全加起来，算得平均原子质量大约是 207.2 u。查元素周期表这个数值和铅（Pb）完全吻合，所以该元素就是铅

#### respond

The result and identification are correct. The weighted sum is approximately 207.2169 u, which is reported as 207.2 u; this matches lead (Pb). In the calculation, each percentage is used as a fractional abundance (for example, 1.40% = 0.0140).

#### reflection

The learner correctly used isotope abundances to form a weighted average and used the periodic-table value to identify Pb. The main point to keep explicit in written work is converting each percentage into a decimal weight before multiplying. This answer provides good evidence for this one application; an independent isotope problem would check transfer.

### Learning record — 2026-10-21 — Exercise 46: iron mass to moles and atoms

Both results are correct: 500.0 g ÷ 55.85 g/mol = 8.95255… mol, reported as 8.953 mol; multiplying by Avogadro's number gives approximately 5.391 × 10²⁴ Fe atoms. The units cancel in sequence from grams to moles to atoms.

### Learning record — 2026-10-21 — Exercise 49: molar masses of Al₂O₃ and Na₃AlF₆

#### question

氧化铝由两个铝和三个氧组成，加起来算出来摩尔质量是 101.96 g/mol，冰晶石含三个钠，一个铝和六个氟，把原子量全累加起来摩尔质量是 209.95 g/mol

#### respond

Both molar masses are correct. Al₂O₃: 2(26.98) + 3(16.00) = 101.96 g/mol. Na₃AlF₆: 3(22.99) + 26.98 + 6(19.00) = 209.95 g/mol.

#### reflection

The explanation names the atom counts as well as the numerical results, which makes the calculation checkable. Further practice can vary the formula while retaining this same counting process.

### Learning record — 2026-10-21 — Check Out 1: weighted average is not an individual isotope mass

#### question

加权平均算下来是 10.0*0.2 + 11.0*0.8 = 10.8 u。这只是个统计上的平均值，自然界里其实根本不存在单个质量是 10.8 u 的原子，随便挑一个出来要么是 10.0 u 要么是 11u

#### respond

The calculation and explanation are correct for the hypothetical isotope masses and abundances in the question. The abundance-weighted average is 10.8 u, while an individual atom belongs to one isotope or the other; the average is not an additional isotope.

#### reflection

The learner understands the distinction between an isotope's individual mass and the population's weighted average. The answer transfers the idea from the lead isotope problem to a new hypothetical element. The masses were given as 10.0 u and 11.0 u, so keep that stated precision when writing the conclusion.

### Learning record — 2026-10-21 — Check Out 3: equal mole amounts of water and carbon dioxide

#### question

两者分子数相同，因为摩尔数都是 0.500 mol，算出来都是 3.01*10^23 个分子。但质量不同，水是 9.01 g，二氧化碳是 22.0 g。“同样多的分子一定一样重”肯定不成立，因为总质量是粒子数和单个粒子质量共同决定的，虽然分子总数一样，但每个二氧化碳分子比水分子重得多

#### respond

The answer is correct. Each 0.500 mol sample contains 3.01 × 10²³ molecules. Their masses differ: H₂O is 9.01 g and CO₂ is 22.0 g. Equal numbers of particles do not imply equal total mass when the particles have different masses.

#### reflection

The learner correctly separated particle count from sample mass and used molar mass to explain the difference. The response includes both calculations and the underlying reason, rather than only stating that the masses differ. A future comparison should preserve this distinction between “same number of particles” and “same mass.”

2. 一份葡萄糖 C₆H₁₂O₆ 样品的质量为 3.60 g。使用 C = 12.01、H = 1.008、O = 16.00 的原子质量数值和 Nₐ = 6.022 × 10²³ mol⁻¹，先求摩尔质量，再求物质的量和葡萄糖分子数。写出换算关系与单位，最后两项结果保留 3 位有效数字。
answer:
葡萄糖摩尔质量按给的数值算是 180.16 g/mol，物质的量用 3.60 g 除以 180.16 g/mol 得 0.0200 mol，然后再乘上 6.022*10^23，得到的分子数是 1.20*10^22 个

3. 0.500 mol H₂O 与 0.500 mol CO₂ 含有的分子数是否相同？质量是否相同？已知两者摩尔质量分别为 18.02 g/mol 和 44.01 g/mol，计算质量并用“粒子数”和“每摩尔的质量”解释，判断“同样多的分子一定一样重”是否成立。
answer:
两者分子数相同，因为摩尔数都是 0.500 mol，算出来都是 3.01*10^23 个分子。但质量不同，水是 9.01 g，二氧化碳是 22.0 g。“同样多的分子一定一样重”肯定不成立，因为总质量是粒子数和单个粒子质量共同决定的，虽然分子总数一样，但每个二氧化碳分子比水分子重得多
